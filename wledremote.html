WLED Remote

A slim touchscreen that controls WLED lights over Wi-Fi.

A 2.8″ ESP32 CYD touchscreen (ESP32-2432S028R) in a slim line case, running BinaryThrottle’s WLED Remote firmware. Landscape by default, with a portrait toggle. Optional desk mount and wall mount.
