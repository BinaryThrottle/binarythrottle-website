Camper Power

Live batteries, solar, DC-DC chargers and diesel heater on a 2.8″ touchscreen.

Firmware for the ESP32 Cheap Yellow Display (ESP32-2432S028R). It monitors campervan power systems over Bluetooth.

Setup

Adding a system

Add each battery, solar controller, DC-DC charger, or diesel heater you want on the display. Up to four Bluetooth devices can be connected at once, including more than one of the same type.

Autodetect

Use Autodetect scan to find supported devices.

Victron key

Instant Readout needs the encryption key from Victron Connect. Copy it in the app, then send it to the display over the setup Wi-Fi.

  1. Open Victron Connect App on your mobile: Select Device.
  2. Cog / settings → 3-dot menu → Product info → Instant readout via Bluetooth → Show encryption data.
  3. Copy the key.
  4. On the CYD, tap AP.
  5. Join the CamperPower-XXXX Wi-Fi with the password shown on the display.
  6. Open http://192.168.4.1 and paste the key, or tap EDIT to type it.

Heater safety

Follow Heater Guidelines. Vent, Use CO alarm.

Troubleshooting

If it doesn’t start at power-on until you press reset, the supply is likely rising too slowly (EN capacitor / supervisor). Use a clean, fast-rising 5V supply rated 1A or more.
